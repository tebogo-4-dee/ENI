What is
ENI Group?

ENI exists to help enterprises and institutions move from need to solution, and from opportunity to execution — connecting the right capability, structure, partner, resource or subsidiary to the requirement in front of it.

Enterprise & Business

Consulting, compliance support, brokerage and enterprise advisory across sectors and scales.

People & Capability

Training, development, talent access, opportunity placement and institutional learning pathways.

Capital & Growth

Enterprise finance, venture investment, sourcing coordination and supply solutions.

Partnerships & Impact

Corporate, institutional, programme, funding, knowledge and in-kind partnerships that advance enterprise and community impact.

Engagement & Influence

Media, communications, convening, networking and open conversations that build ecosystem visibility.
